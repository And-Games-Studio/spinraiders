Spin Raiders

Spin. Build. Raid.

Spin the slot for coins, shields and bonus spins, and build your village up to the next one: five villages to build, from Mossy Hollow to the Skyroot Citadel.

Win an attack and hit another player's village, or win a raid and dig for their coins. Pay back whoever raided you, send your friends a free spin every day, and fill ten card albums.

Spin Raiders is free to play. It has no real-money prizes: coins, spins and cards stay in the game and can't be cashed out.
