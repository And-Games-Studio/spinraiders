Spin Raiders

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

In short: Spin Raiders keeps your game on our own server, so you can raid other players and they can raid you. Other players see your name and your village. You don't sign up: the game makes an account for you with a random identifier. It shows rewarded videos through Google AdMob, which you choose to watch; in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland it asks for your consent before ads use your personal data. You can buy spins and coins through Google Play or the App Store, which handle the payment. Usage statistics go to a third party only if you turn them on. The game doesn't ask for your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone, and on iPhone it doesn't ask to track you. You can download or delete your data at any time in Settings.

Who we are

Spin Raiders is made by Andrea Ebano, publishing as And Games, based in Portugal. Andrea Ebano is the controller of the personal data described here. Contact: ebano.andrea.games@gmail.com.

What data the game uses, and why

Your account

The first time you open the game, it creates an account on our server, tied to a random identifier made on your phone for this installation (not your phone's hardware ID). The account has a name the game makes up for you, for example "Bold Otter 11", and a friend code. There is no email or password.

Your game

Everything you play is kept on our server, because the server decides every spin, attack and raid (so nobody can cheat): your coins, spins, shields and shards; your villages and buildings; your cards, chests and albums; your daily bonus; the attacks and raids you have waiting; and a record of the purchases and rewarded videos given to you, so each is given once.

Raiding and being raided

Other players can be sent to attack or raid your village, and you theirs. They see your name and your village (which buildings you have and how damaged they are). When someone attacks or raids you, your raid log shows their name, what they did and the coins you lost, for 30 days; their log shows yours when you hit back. To pick who you meet, the server keeps a short index of players: village level, how much is built, when they last played and when they were last hit.

Friends and gifts

You can add a friend with their friend code; you become friends when they add you back. Friends see your name. You can send each friend a free spin a day; gifts wait for them on the server for up to 7 days.

Tournaments

During a tournament event, your raids or attacks earn points. The leaderboard shows every player's name, rank and points, until the tournament ends.

Notifications

The game can remind you when your spins are full again: that reminder is made on your phone. It can also tell you when another player attacked or raided you, or a friend sent you a spin: for that your phone gives the game a notification token, which we keep with your account and send, with the message, to Google's Firebase Cloud Messaging (on iPhone it passes it to Apple's push service). You can turn notifications off in your phone's settings.

Purchases (Google Play, App Store)

You can buy spins and coins in the game's shop. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play (or by Apple's App Store on iPhone): we never see your card or other payment details. The store gives the game a receipt, which our server checks with Google or Apple before giving you what you bought; we keep the receipt's transaction details (product, time, transaction number) so each purchase is given once. Chests and cards are never sold for real money. Unity's purchasing library, which the game uses to talk to the store, also reports purchases to Unity Technologies (the product, its price, the time, the store's receipt and basic details of your phone and the game). Google and Apple handle your purchase as their own privacy policies describe.

Rewarded videos (Google AdMob)

The shop offers a video you can choose to watch for a few free spins. Videos are shown by Google AdMob. To show and measure them, and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads software in the game sends Google: your phone's advertising ID (on Android; you can reset or delete it in your phone's settings, under Google → Ads), and on iPhone an identifier for this app's vendor; your IP address, from which an approximate location can be worked out; information about your device and the app; how you interact with the ads; and your consent choices. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland a consent form from Google appears before the first video; ads are personalised only if you agree, and you can change your choice in Settings. The game asks Google only for ads suitable for most audiences. We never see your advertising ID; we only get totals, such as how many videos were watched. Our server counts the videos given to you each day, so it can stop at the daily limit.

Usage statistics (optional)

If you turn on "Anonymous usage statistics" (when the game first opens, or later in Settings), the game sends GameAnalytics events about how you play: sessions, spins, when you run out of spins, purchases, with a random identifier and basic details of your phone. It's off unless you turn it on, and you can turn it off at any time. Your choice and the date are recorded on our server.

Statistics on our server

To keep the game running well and fair, our server counts, for each player and day, the sessions, spins, times you ran out of spins, purchase amounts and videos. We use these counts only in totals (for example how many players come back after a week, or how much the game earns per player) and never sell or share them.

Reports

If you write to us about another player, we use what you send only to deal with it, and keep it at most 12 months.

Technical data

When the game connects to our server, the server sees your IP address and keeps it in its logs for at most 14 days, to keep the service working and secure. The Unity engine may send Unity anonymous information about your device when the game starts.

Legal bases

We use your account, your game, raids, friends, gifts, tournaments, notifications and purchases to provide the game and the features you use (Article 6(1)(b) of the GDPR). Server logs, the player index, the statistics on our server and reports rest on our legitimate interest in running a fair, secure and working game (Article 6(1)(f)). Personalised ads and storing or reading information on your phone for ads rest on your consent (Article 6(1)(a)), which you can withdraw in Settings; non-personalised ads, their measurement and fraud prevention rest on legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f)). Usage statistics sent to GameAnalytics rest on your consent (Article 6(1)(a)).

How long we keep it

Your account and your game stay until you delete your account; if you stop playing, we may delete an account that has been inactive for 24 months. Raid log entries are kept for 30 days (at most the last 50), gifts for 7 days, server logs for 14 days. Our server backups are encrypted and overwritten within 5 weeks, so deleted data leaves them by then. Your consent choices for ads stay on your phone until you change them or clear the game's data; Google keeps ad data for the periods set out in its privacy policy.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, to restrict or object to its use, and to receive it in a portable format. In the game, Settings → Download my data gives you a copy of everything our server keeps about you, and Settings → Delete my account deletes it (see Delete your data). You can also write to ebano.andrea.games@gmail.com. To withdraw your consent to ads or statistics, use Settings in the game; to stop ads being linked to your phone, reset or delete its advertising ID in the phone's settings. You can also complain to a data protection authority: in Portugal, the CNPD (cnpd.pt), or the one where you live.

Children

Spin Raiders is meant for players aged 13 and over. We don't knowingly collect data from children under 13; if you think a child has played, write to us and we'll delete the account.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
