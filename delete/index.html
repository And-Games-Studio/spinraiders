Delete your data

You can delete your Spin Raiders account and all its data at any time.

In the game

Open Spin Raiders, tap Settings on the spin screen, then Delete my account, and tap it again to confirm. Everything is deleted from our server straight away, and the game starts again as a new player. In the same place, Download my data gives you a copy of everything we keep about you.

Don't have the game anymore? Email ebano.andrea.games@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data" and your friend code (the game shows it under Friends) or your player name. We delete your account and its data within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What gets deleted

  • Your player account: your name, your friend code and the random identifier of your phone's installation.
  • Your game: coins, spins, shields, your villages, your cards and chests, shards, daily bonus and the record of what you bought and of the videos you watched.
  • Your raid log, the free spins your friends sent you, and your friends list. You disappear from your friends' lists too.
  • Your scores in tournaments.
  • The notification tokens of your phones, and your privacy choices.
  • The daily statistics kept about your play (sessions, spins, purchases).
  • In other players' raid logs, your name is replaced with "Deleted player".

What stays

  • Copies in our encrypted server backups, which are overwritten within 5 weeks.
  • The record of your purchases kept by Google Play or the App Store, which belongs to them: you manage it in your store account.
  • Totals that don't identify anyone, such as how many players were active on a day.

Deleted data can't be recovered: a new account starts from the first village, and purchases made with the old one can't be moved to it. Details are in our privacy policy.
